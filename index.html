What is
ZENO

ZENO is an exploration of intelligent daily living. It focuses on the small patterns that shape how people move through their days — routines, rest, attention, and the quiet space between tasks.

The project asks a simple question: how can an intelligent presence support modern life without adding to its noise?

Observing routines and daily habits without judgment
Exploring calm assistance and intelligent awareness
Documenting how AI can quietly support — not interrupt — modern life
